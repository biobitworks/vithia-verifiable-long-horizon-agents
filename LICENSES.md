# Licensing and rights scope

This public hackathon repository keeps software licensing separate from governed content rights.

- Software components derived from the Vitaology implementation retain their applicable Apache-2.0 software licensing.
- User-owned governed submission artifacts are published under the project CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 rights policy unless a specific submission/platform term controls that copy.
- Third-party source material is not redistributed merely because a citation or content hash is referenced.
- Hashes, fingerprints, signatures, Git commits, and Merkle/MMR inclusion establish identity/custody properties; they do not themselves create ownership, prove truth, or establish causality.
- Private keys and provider credentials are not part of the public package.
